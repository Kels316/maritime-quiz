# Content notes

The ten source-guide errors and inconsistencies found while building the quiz (towing-guide fog signals, fishing lights, compass memory aid, one-prolonged-blast signal, code flag B, petrol ignition temperature, rope safety factor, drill frequency, extinguisher service intervals) were corrected in the learner's guides on 14 Oct 2026, and the quiz now follows the corrected guides.

Open items:

- The MRQ Vessel Crew Guide is draft v0.1. MRQ-specific questions (alcohol limit, phone use, sitrep timing, AMSA Form 19, flags D and R-over-Y) may need updating when v1.0 is issued.
- Petrol/diesel ignition temperatures in the Propulsion guide were changed to about 280 °C and 210 °C respectively; check against your reference.
- The Vuee Tuee sound-signal table lists "3 long: person overboard", which is not a COLREGS signal.
