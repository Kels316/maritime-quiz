# Content notes – where the source guides need a second look

While building the question bank, these inconsistencies turned up. The quiz uses the authoritative version (usually the COLREGS text) and cites the guide; the guides may want fixing.

| # | Guide | What it says | Issue / what the quiz uses |
|---|-------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| 1 | GPH Part 1 – lights table (fishing) | "Two all-round lights… green over white (trawling…)" for *vessel engaged in fishing* | Green over white is **trawling** only. Fishing other than trawling is **red over white** (Rule 26). |
| 2 | NATRAC002 Towing guide §1.6 (Rule 35) | Towing vessel: two short + one long; towed vessel: three short + one long | Rule 35(c)/(e): towing vessel **one prolonged + two short**; last vessel in the tow **one prolonged + three short**. (Same guide's own GPH Part 1 table has it right for the tug.) |
| 3 | Marine SAR guide – "CADET" memory aid | "Errors EAST are added going from True to Compass" | Reversed. Going **True → Compass subtract East / add West**; Compass → True add East. (Passage Planning guide has it right.) |
| 4 | Drill frequency | GPH Part 1: about every 2 months; Fire guide: quarterly; MRQ Crew Guide v0.1: at least every 6 months | Three different intervals. No quiz question asks for a number. |
| 5 | Factor of safety for fibre rope / rigging | GPH Part 1: FOS 5; Towing guide: "at least 6", plus WLL = D²/200 in the quick reference | Quiz uses each guide's own figure and says which. Suggest aligning them. |
| 6 | GPH Part 2 – Code "B" flag | "a solid red flag" | Code flag B is a red **swallow-tailed** flag. |
| 7 | GPH Part 1 – sound signals | "1 prolonged blast: vessel leaving a berth…" | Not a COLREGS signal. One prolonged blast is used for a vessel approaching a blind bend (Rule 34(e)) and by a power-driven vessel making way in restricted visibility (Rule 35(a)). |
| 8 | Fire aide-memoire vs Fire guide v2.3 | Aide-memoire: service tags 6-monthly, annual, 3-yearly, 6-yearly; Guide: 6-monthly (crew), 12-monthly and five-yearly (accredited) | Quiz follows guide v2.3 (AS 1851-2012 / AMSA 707). |
| 9 | Propulsion guide – petrol vs diesel table | Petrol "ignition temperature ≈ −35 °C" | That figure looks like petrol's flashpoint (≈ −40 °C); petrol's auto-ignition temperature is several hundred °C. Not used in the quiz. |
| 10 | MRQ Vessel Crew Guide | Version 0.1 | Draft – MRQ-specific questions may need updating when v1.0 is issued. |
